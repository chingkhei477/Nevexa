How Nevexa cashback works

Partner stores pay Nevexa a commission when you buy through our link. When the store confirms your order, we share part of that commission with you as cashback.

The four steps

  1. 1

    Choose an offer

    Open a partner store's offer on Nevexa. Read the cashback rate, eligible products, exclusions and expected confirmation time before you shop.

    Takes a minute
  2. 2

    Shop through Nevexa

    Click through to the store and complete your purchase in the same browser session, following the offer's conditions. The store records that you came from Nevexa.

    Usually tracked within 72 hours
  3. 3

    Wait for store confirmation

    Your cashback stays pending while the store's return and cancellation window runs. Once the store approves the order, the cashback is confirmed.

    Typically 30–90 days
  4. 4

    Withdraw your cashback

    Transfer confirmed cashback to a UPI ID or Indian bank account in your name. Nevexa charges no withdrawal fee.

    Usually within 7 business days

Reward statuses

You'll see one of these on every reward.

Nevexa reward statuses
StatusMeaning
TrackedThe store has reported your purchase to Nevexa.
PendingThe store's return and validation period is running. The amount can change if items are returned.
ConfirmedThe store approved the order. The cashback is in your withdrawable balance.
DeclinedThe store didn't approve the order, for example after a return, cancellation or a condition not being met. We show the reason where the store provides one.
PaidYou've withdrawn the cashback to your UPI ID or bank account.

Make sure your purchase tracks

Stores pay commission only when they can see the visit came from Nevexa.

Do

  • Start from Nevexa each time you shop
  • Complete the purchase in the same visit and browser
  • Allow cookies while shopping
  • Use only coupons listed on the Nevexa offer
  • Keep your order confirmation until cashback is confirmed

Avoid

  • Switching to the store's app unless app orders are included
  • Visiting other coupon or cashback sites before buying
  • Ad blockers or settings that block tracking
  • Finishing a cart you started before clicking through
  • Paying with excluded methods like gift cards or store credit

Purchase not tracked?

If your purchase hasn't appeared within 72 hours, contact us within 30 days of the purchase date. Include the store name, order ID, order date and amount. We'll raise it with the store and update you. The store makes the final decision on missing claims.

Report missing cashback
Why is my cashback lower than expected?

Cashback is calculated on the order value the store reports, usually excluding taxes, delivery, fees and discounts. Partial returns reduce it.

Can an offer change?

Yes. Stores can change or end offers at any time. The rate shown when you purchased applies, subject to the store's confirmation.

Is there a minimum withdrawal?

A minimum amount may apply. If it does, it's shown before you request a withdrawal.
