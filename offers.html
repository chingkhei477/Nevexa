Five things every Nevexa offer shows

Check them before you click through to a store.

Cashback rate

A percentage or fixed amount. It can vary by category or customer type.

What qualifies

Eligible products, platforms (web or app) and payment methods.

Exclusions

Brands, items, coupons or payment methods that won't earn cashback.

Tracking time

How soon the store usually reports your purchase.

Confirmation time

The typical wait before cashback is withdrawable.

Conditions on every offer

  • You pay the store's normal price; Nevexa adds nothing
  • Stores can change or end offers without notice
  • The rate shown at the time of purchase applies
  • Cashback is paid only after the store confirms the order
  • Any bonus rewards carry their own stated terms

Eligibility depends on offer terms

Not every purchase earns cashback. Each offer's conditions and our Cashback & Rewards Terms decide eligibility. Not sure if a purchase qualifies? Ask us first.
