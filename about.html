About Nevexa

Nevexa is an India-focused cashback and rewards platform based in New Delhi. We help shoppers get back part of what they spend at partner stores online.

What we do

Online stores pay a referral commission when a customer arrives from a partner website and makes a purchase. Nevexa is that partner. We share part of the commission with you as cashback, so everyday shopping costs a little less.

We built Nevexa around one idea: you should know exactly what to expect before you buy. That's why every offer lists its conditions and every reward has a status you can follow.

How Nevexa earns

Our revenue comes from commissions paid by partner stores for confirmed sales. Shoppers pay nothing to join, shop or withdraw, and we don't sell personal data.

Because stores pay us only for confirmed orders, we pay cashback only on confirmed orders.

Our commitments

Transparency

Rates, exclusions and timelines are stated on every offer in plain language.

Honest promises

We never promise income or guaranteed returns. Cashback is a reward on eligible spending.

Responsive support

Our support team replies to every message within 2 business days.

Business details

Nevexa serves shoppers across India from New Delhi. Store names on Nevexa belong to their owners and are used only to show where an offer applies.
